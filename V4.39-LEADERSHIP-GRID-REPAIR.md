# v4.39 — Leadership Grid Repair

## Purpose
Correct the desktop leadership team layout after v4.38 rendered the seven leadership cards as a six-column grid. That caused the Chairman card to share the first row with two other leaders and made the remaining cards unnecessarily small.

## Required layout
- Row 1: Otunba Olusola Adekanola alone, centred.
- Row 2: Dr. Julius Olugbade (PhD.), Kehinde Oyeleke, Mr. Bamidele Martins.
- Row 3: Success Igboekwere, John Adeniyi Dairo, Udon Joel Nkeki.

## Implementation
- Desktop leadership grid changed to three equal columns.
- Chairman card spans the full first grid row and is centred at a maximum width of 430px.
- Rows 2 and 3 contain three equal-width cards.
- Leadership titles remain bold.
- Tablet uses two columns while keeping the Chairman alone on row 1.
- Mobile uses one card per row.
- Existing supplied portrait files and leadership profile data are preserved.
