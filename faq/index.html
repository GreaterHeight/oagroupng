About OA Group

OAGroupNG is the parent digital hub for OA Group, a diversified Nigerian business group comprising five CAC-registered subsidiary entities across accounting and professional services, management consulting, real estate, financial services and contracting.

The build prompt identifies OA Group and its five subsidiaries as operating in Nigeria and being CAC-registered. The site should be used as a corporate group hub; entity-specific statutory records should be confirmed with the relevant entity where a formal verification is required.

The parent group currently presents five subsidiary entities: Olusola Adekanola & Co, OA Consulting Limited, Henrisol Properties Ltd, Maria Oyinlola Limited (MOL), and Pearl Primus Multipurpose Contracting Limited.

The group spans Accounting & Audit, Management Consulting, Real Estate, Financial Services, Contracting and Procurement-related commercial activity.

The supplied build brief positions OA Group as a Nigerian group. It does not provide evidence of an established operating footprint outside Nigeria, so international availability should be confirmed directly with the group.
