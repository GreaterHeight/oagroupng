About OA Group

OAGroupNG — commonly referred to as OA Group — is a diversified Nigerian business group comprising five independently CAC-registered subsidiary entities. The group operates across Accounting and Professional Services, Management Consulting, Real Estate, Financial Services and Microfinance, and Contracting and Procurement. OA Group functions as a holding identity: providing shared governance, professional standards, and brand equity to each entity whilst allowing each to develop genuine sector-specific depth and expertise.
All OA Group entities are registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC). The group operates from Lagos, Nigeria, and serves clients across the country. CAC registration means each entity meets Nigeria's legal requirements for corporate formation, governance, and operations — providing clients with regulatory assurance about the entities they engage.
OA Group currently comprises five subsidiary entities: (1) Olusola Adekanola & Co — Accounting & Professional Services; (2) OA Consulting Limited — Management Consulting & Advisory; (3) Henrisol Properties Ltd — Real Estate & Property Development; (4) Maria Oyinlola Limited (MOL) — Financial Services & Microfinance; and (5) Pearl Primus Multipurpose Contracting Limited — Contracting, Procurement & Commercial Ventures. Each entity has its own independent CAC registration and dedicated website.
OA Group collectively serves clients across six sectors: Accounting & Audit, Management Consulting, Real Estate, Financial Services, Contracting, and Procurement. Client types include private sector companies (SMEs to large corporates), government ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs), parastatals, regulated institutions, individuals, and diaspora investors. The group has served clients across banking, manufacturing, professional services, public sector, real estate, and financial services industries.
OA Group's entities are incorporated in Nigeria and primarily serve the Nigerian market. However, the group regularly serves international clients — including Nigerian diaspora investors through Henrisol Properties, multinational corporations seeking Nigerian regulatory advisory, and international institutions with Nigerian operations. The group's expertise is Nigeria-focused but client-facing to the global Nigerian diaspora and international business community.

Services

Olusola Adekanola & Co offers a comprehensive range of professional services: Statutory Auditing (independent audit of financial statements for CAC filing and regulatory purposes), Internal Audit (review of internal systems and controls), Accounting Services and Financial Reporting (bookkeeping, management accounts, IFRS-compliant financial statements), Internal Control and Compliance, Risk Management, Tax Services and Advisory (FIRS-compliant tax planning, computation, and filing), Liquidation and Insolvency services, and Company Secretariat services (CAC filings, board documentation, statutory registers). OA & Co is ICAN-affiliated and operates to the standards required for regulated professional accounting practice in Nigeria.
Revenue Recovery consulting is OA Consulting's practice of identifying, quantifying, and recovering revenue that organisations are losing through process failure, undercollection, leakage, or ineffective billing and collection systems. For government agencies and parastatals, this frequently involves analysing billing systems, payment channels, enforcement mechanisms, and regulatory compliance — then implementing systematic improvements to close the gap between assessed and collected revenue. OA Consulting deploys structured diagnostic methodology, stakeholder engagement, and performance tracking to deliver measurable, auditable recovery outcomes.

Process & Compliance

Yes. All five OA Group entities — Olusola Adekanola & Co, OA Consulting Limited, Henrisol Properties Ltd, Maria Oyinlola Limited, and Pearl Primus Multipurpose Contracting Limited — are independently registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission (CAC). CAC registration is a baseline requirement, not a differentiator, for OA Group entities. Clients can request CAC registration confirmation for any entity as part of their due diligence process.
Client confidentiality is a foundational professional obligation across all OA Group entities — not merely a policy choice. All engagement letters include confidentiality provisions. Staff are bound by professional and contractual confidentiality requirements. Where cross-entity referral occurs, client consent is obtained before any information is shared. OA Group's data handling practices comply with Nigeria's NDPR (Nigeria Data Protection Regulation) and applicable international data protection standards for international clients. Data access requests should be directed to dpo@oagroupng.com.
The typical engagement process begins with an initial enquiry — submitted via the Contact form, email, or telephone. A qualified professional from the appropriate entity will respond within 48 hours to discuss your needs. Following the initial conversation, the entity will prepare an engagement proposal covering scope of work, fees, timeline, and key terms. Upon acceptance, a formal engagement letter is issued and signed before work commences. Throughout the engagement, the client is kept informed of progress, findings, and recommendations in a format agreed at the outset.
Professional indemnity and liability coverage is a standard requirement for professional service providers operating at OA Group's level. Specific insurance details for individual engagement types and entities can be confirmed during the engagement proposal process. Clients requiring evidence of professional indemnity coverage as part of their procurement or risk management requirements should raise this during initial discussions and the relevant documentation will be provided.
For formal complaints regarding services received from any OA Group entity, please write to info@oagroupng.com with the subject line "Formal Complaint" and include the entity name, engagement details, and nature of the complaint. We will acknowledge within 48 hours and respond substantively within 10 working days. For data access requests, corrections, or deletion requests under the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) or GDPR, please write to dpo@oagroupng.com. For more information, see our Privacy Policy.
