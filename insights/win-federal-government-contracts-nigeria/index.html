How to Win Federal Government Contracts in Nigeria

Government contracting rewards preparation. Procurement readiness, documentation, capability evidence and disciplined tendering all matter.

By OA Group Editorial

Winning public-sector contracts begins before the tender notice. Organisations that consistently compete well tend to maintain procurement readiness: current documentation, defined capabilities, credible evidence of delivery and an internal process for reviewing opportunities.

The tender itself should be treated as a compliance and commercial exercise. Read the requirements literally, identify mandatory documents, map evaluation criteria and assign ownership for every response.

Evidence matters. Buyers need to understand what the contractor can actually deliver, how the work will be managed and what controls exist around quality, timelines, reporting and risk.

BPP-related requirements and procurement rules should be addressed using specific, current information. Organisations should avoid vague claims about registration or compliance.
