The OA Group Integrated Approach: Why One Group Can Serve the Whole Business Lifecycle

The value of a diversified group is created when specialist capabilities remain distinct but can be coordinated around the client’s wider problem.

By OA Group Editorial

A diversified group can become either a collection of unrelated businesses or a genuinely useful institution. The difference is architecture. OA Group’s model keeps five specialist companies distinct while creating a parent platform through which their capabilities can be understood and, where appropriate, coordinated.

The group website therefore has a different job from a subsidiary website. It explains the institution, presents the portfolio, shows where capabilities combine and provides a clear route for enquiries that do not fit neatly into one box.

For clients, the benefit is reduced fragmentation. A business challenge can begin as an accounting issue, become an advisory issue, require financing, involve property or move into project execution.

The model also protects specialist credibility. Each operating company retains its own proposition, clients, technical expertise and regulatory identity.
