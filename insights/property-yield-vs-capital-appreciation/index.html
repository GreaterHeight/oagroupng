Property Yield vs Capital Appreciation: Making the Right Investment Choice in Nigeria

A property investment decision should distinguish recurring income from long-term value growth and assess each against the investor’s objectives.

By OA Group Editorial

Property investors often compare yield and capital appreciation as if one must replace the other. In practice, the right balance depends on the asset, location, financing structure, holding period and investor objective.

Yield focuses on recurring income relative to the capital committed. It can be especially relevant where the investor needs cash flow, but a high headline yield does not automatically mean a strong investment if vacancy, maintenance, financing or management costs materially reduce the net outcome.

Capital appreciation is the potential increase in asset value over time. It can be attractive for long-term investors, but it is less predictable and may require patience. Location fundamentals, infrastructure, demand, supply and asset quality all influence the potential.

A useful investment assessment therefore models both income and value growth, then tests the assumptions.
