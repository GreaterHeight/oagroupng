Olusola Adekanola & Co

OA & Co — Chartered Accountants & Professional Services

Olusola Adekanola & Co is the founding entity of OA Group and its primary accounting practice. Built on over four decades of ICAN-accredited professional service, OA & Co provides statutory audit, internal control, and compliance services to companies across the private and public sectors of the Nigerian economy.

The practice operates at the intersection of regulatory compliance and business advisory — bringing not only technical audit competence, but actionable insight into operational and financial risk management that translates into direct client value.

CAC-registered | ICAN-affiliated accounting practice

Services Offered

  • Statutory Auditing
  • Internal Audit
  • Accounting Services & Financial Reporting
  • Internal Control & Compliance
  • Risk Management
  • Tax Services & Advisory
  • Liquidation & Insolvency
  • Company Secretariat Services

OA Consulting Limited

OA Consulting Limited — Management Advisory & Strategy

OA Consulting Limited is OA Group's management advisory arm, specialising in the areas of business transformation, revenue optimisation, and organisational capacity development. The firm works with private sector clients, government ministries, departments and agencies (MDAs), and institutional organisations across Nigeria.

A defining capability of OA Consulting is its Revenue Recovery practice — one of Nigeria's most pressing advisory needs for government institutions, statutory agencies, and revenue-generating enterprises where leakage, undercollection, and process failure represent significant and recoverable value.

CAC-registered management advisory company

Services Offered

  • Business Management Advisory
  • Revenue Generation & Recovery
  • Training & Capacity Development
  • Merger, Amalgamation & Acquisition Advisory
  • Procurement Management
  • Cost & Process Optimisation
  • Debt Recovery

Real Estate & Property Development

Henrisol Properties Ltd

Henrisol Properties Limited — Real Estate & Property Development

Henrisol Properties Ltd is OA Group's real estate arm, delivering investment-grade property services to individual, diaspora, and institutional clients. Operating across the Nigerian property market — with particular focus on residential, commercial, and mixed-use developments — Henrisol brings both development expertise and investment advisory capability to its clients.

The Nigerian property market presents some of Africa's most compelling investment opportunities, alongside some of its most complex legal and title-verification challenges. Henrisol's value proposition is grounded in navigating that complexity safely and profitably on behalf of clients — including a robust diaspora investment service that enables Nigerians abroad to invest securely in local property.

CAC-registered real estate company

Services Offered

  • Real Estate Investment Advisory
  • Property Development & Management
  • Facilities Management
  • Sales of Landed Properties
  • Sales of Housing Properties
  • Diaspora Property Investment Services
  • Property Title Verification & Due Diligence

Maria Oyinlola Limited (MOL)

Maria Oyinlola Limited — Financial Services & Microfinance

Maria Oyinlola Limited (MOL) is OA Group's financial services entity, providing credit, lending, advisory, and investment services to individuals, SMEs, and institutional clients. MOL operates at the intersection of traditional financial advisory and modern digital financing — addressing the persistent access-to-finance gap that constrains Nigerian economic growth.

MOL's corporate and institutional financial services capability extends the entity's mandate beyond retail finance into the structured financial needs of larger organisations — including asset management, investment advisory, and revenue distribution services for institutional clients.

CAC-registered financial services company | Operations within CBN guidelines

Services Offered

  • Credit & Lending
  • Financial Advisory
  • Investment & Asset Management
  • Agency & Revenue/Payment Distribution
  • Digital Financing Solutions
  • Corporate & Institutional Financial Services
  • SME Finance Advisory

Contracting, Procurement & Commercial

Pearl Primus Contracting

Pearl Primus Multipurpose Contracting Limited

Pearl Primus Multipurpose Contracting Limited is OA Group's contracting and commercial ventures entity. It operates in the intersection of investment management, contract administration, and project facilitation — serving both government and private sector clients across Nigeria's broad contracting landscape.

Pearl Primus brings particular expertise in government procurement — advising clients on BPP compliance, registration, bid strategy, and contract execution. Its commercial ventures capability also extends to partnerships, joint ventures, and investment facilitation in sectors where project execution and commercial structuring are the critical success factors.

CAC-registered contracting company | BPP framework-compliant

Services Offered

  • Investment Management
  • Contract Administration
  • Project Facilitation
  • Procurement Management & Advisory
  • Government Contracting (BPP-compliant)
  • Commercial Ventures
  • Joint Venture Structuring

Integrated Value

How our entities work together

OA Group's integrated model creates cross-entity value that no single specialist firm can replicate. These are examples of how our entities collaborate to serve clients more completely.

From Entity To Entity Integration Pathway Client Benefit
OA & Co (Audit) OA Consulting (Advisory) Audit findings and control gaps feed directly into OA Consulting's improvement advisory mandate Clients receive not just an audit report but an actionable improvement roadmap from the same trusted group
Henrisol (Property) MOL (Finance) Property investment opportunities identified by Henrisol can be financed through MOL's credit and investment facilities Clients can identify, assess, and finance real estate investments through a single group relationship
Pearl Primus (Contracting) OA Consulting (Advisory) Contracting clients receive procurement management advisory and cost-optimisation support from OA Consulting alongside Pearl Primus's contract administration Superior contract outcomes and cost control through integrated procurement and advisory support
OA & Co (Audit) MOL (Finance) Audited financial statements prepared by OA & Co support MOL's credit assessment process for lending clients Clients benefit from audit-quality financial data that strengthens their credit applications
OA Consulting (M&A) OA & Co (Audit) Merger and acquisition mandates at OA Consulting engage OA & Co for due diligence, audit, and regulatory compliance verification End-to-end M&A support from deal structuring through independent financial verification
