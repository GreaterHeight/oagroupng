The challenge

Certificate administration should not depend on queues, paper and disconnected records.

eCertMan® addresses the operational friction created by manual certificate requests, paper-based processing, disconnected payment reconciliation and limited management visibility.

For candidates

A secure self-service route for initiating requests, tracking status and receiving material notifications rather than relying entirely on physical visits and repeated enquiries.

For institutions

A governed process connecting verification, payment, fulfilment, collection or delivery, audit and management intelligence in one controlled environment.

How it works

One governed process, end to end.

Every stage is connected so the institution can understand where a request is, what has happened and what should happen next.

01Request

Candidate initiates a certificate request through the secure self-service portal.

02Verify

Examination records are verified against approved institutional data sources.

03Pay

Payment is processed through approved online or offline channels.

04Process

The request enters the institutional processing workflow.

05Allocate

The certificate is allocated and tracked through fulfilment.

06Notify

The candidate is notified of status at material events.

07Collect / Deliver

Collection occurs at approved locations or through approved delivery.

08Audit

A full audit trail is generated for every transaction.

09Analyse

Management intelligence is surfaced across operational and financial KPIs.
