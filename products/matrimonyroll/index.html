The vision

A digital operating environment for marriage administration.

MatrimonyRoll™ moves beyond simply putting paper processes online. It connects applications, workflows, secure payments, records, certificates, verification, reporting and institutional administration.

Digital Applications→Workflow Automation→Secure Payments→Records & Certificates→Verification→Analytics

Applicant & couple portal

Make the service journey clearer for applicants.

The platform can provide secure accounts for couples to start and continue applications, submit documents, make payments, view status, receive notifications, schedule appointments where applicable and track certificate requests.

The proposal is deliberately mobile-first, reflecting how many Nigerian applicants access online services.

Solution architecture

One configurable platform across the marriage administration lifecycle.

Each participating organisation can have its own profile, locations, users, roles, services, fees, workflows, forms, notifications, documents, reports and operating rules.

Certificate & verification

Core capabilities

Connected functions across service, records and control.

Marriage applications & notices

Digital biodata, documentation, declarations, validation, review, approval, numbering and status tracking, with configurable notice and objection workflows.

Payments & revenue control

Service fees, online and offline payments, receipts, failed-payment handling, refunds and reconciliation with revenue intelligence.

Document & digital records

Secure document management, searchable archives, historical-record migration and controlled access.

Appointments & ceremonies

Registry locations, dates, time slots, officer availability, ceremony details, attendance and certificate issuance status.

Licensing & institutional participation

Support for places-of-worship licensing and configured church/place-of-worship marriage administration.

Notifications & help desk

Email, SMS and portal notifications, feedback, complaints, case tracking and service communication.

Security & auditability

Granular roles, privileged controls, secure authentication, encryption, logging and complete activity history.

Analytics & reporting

Application, marriage, revenue, operational and certificate intelligence with scheduled and custom reporting.

Institutional intelligence

From records to decision support.

Management dashboards can surface applications, marriages, revenue, processing times, officer workload, certificate activity, complaints and SLA performance.

Advanced deployments can also introduce rules-based anomaly monitoring, data reconciliation, forecasting and other future capabilities subject to scope and approval.

Privacy by design

Verification without unnecessary disclosure.

The source proposal explicitly recommends privacy-preserving verification rather than unrestricted public searches of marital status. Access to sensitive information should be controlled according to law and institutional policy.

Public certificate verification

Where authorised, verification can be limited to certificate number, verification code or QR code and only the information the institution is legally permitted to disclose.

Institutional verification

Approved embassies, institutions and other authorised users can receive controlled verification appropriate to their role and purpose.
