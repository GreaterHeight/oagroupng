About OA Group

Built on professional integrity. Driven by client outcomes. Structured for sustainable impact across Nigeria's economy.

Our Story

From practice to platform.

OA Group's story is the story of professional ambition meeting Nigerian market reality. What began as a focused accounting and professional services practice — built on decades of ICAN-accredited expertise — expanded as client demand for broader, more integrated advisory support grew beyond what a single entity could credibly deliver.

Rather than stretching one firm's mandate beyond its core competence, the founders made a deliberate structural decision: build separate, specialist entities for each discipline, each independently registered with Nigeria's Corporate Affairs Commission, each governed by the same professional standards, but each given the space to develop genuine depth in its sector.

The result is OA Group as it exists today — a holding identity that gives five distinct entities shared governance, shared values, and the credibility of a group whilst preserving each entity's independence and sector focus. Clients benefit from both: they engage a specialist firm, and they access the broader group's network when multi-disciplinary support is needed.

Across five entities and six sectors, OA Group now serves individuals, SMEs, corporations, and government institutions across Nigeria — with a track record built on transparency, integrity, and an unwavering focus on client-outcome delivery.

Core Values

What we stand for

These are not aspirational statements. They are the operating principles that govern every decision across OA Group and each of its entities.

Professionalism

Every client — regardless of size, sector, or fee — receives the same rigorous standards of preparation, execution, and documentation. Professionalism at OA Group is not a posture; it is a baseline.

Integrity

Integrity at OA Group means full transparency in pricing, scope, findings, and recommendations — even when the findings are uncomfortable. We do not selectively report; we do not soften assessments to preserve engagements.

Innovation

Nigerian business is evolving at pace. We invest in developing new service capabilities, adopting appropriate technology, and continuously updating our methodologies to remain genuinely useful — not just conventionally credentialed.

Value Creation

Our ultimate metric is client outcomes. We measure our success by the business growth, cost reductions, compliance improvements, and strategic advantages our clients achieve as a direct result of working with OA Group.

Group Structure

Hub-and-spoke. Integrated by design.

OAGroupNG operates as an umbrella holding identity for five distinct, CAC-registered entities, each specialised in its sector, each benefiting from the group's shared governance and professional standards.

OAGroupNG — Parent Group
Olusola Adekanola & Co Accounting
OA Consulting Limited Consulting
Henrisol Properties Ltd Real Estate
Maria Oyinlola Limited Financial Services
Pearl Primus Contracting Contracting

Each entity operates with sector-specific governance and its own CAC registration, enabling it to build authentic expertise and credibility. The OA Group parent identity provides shared professional standards, governance oversight, brand equity, and cross-referral pathways — ensuring that clients who engage one entity gain access to the full group's capabilities when relevant.
