The Group

About OA Group

A Nigerian group architecture designed to connect specialist expertise, professional governance and practical client outcomes.

Group Story

The front door to a diversified professional group.

OA Group is presented as a diversified Nigerian business group comprising five CAC-registered subsidiary entities operating across accounting and professional services, management consulting, real estate, financial services and contracting.

The parent identity is intentionally different from the specialist websites beneath it. OAGroupNG exists to establish institutional credibility, explain the group structure, communicate governance and direct visitors to the entity best positioned to handle a specific need.

In the Nigerian market, formal corporate registration and professional standing are important signals of accountability. The group therefore foregrounds its CAC-registered structure while keeping entity-specific professional and regulatory claims appropriately tied to the relevant subsidiary.

As the group develops, the parent platform can also serve as a press, partner and investor entry point, creating a coherent narrative around the capabilities of its constituent businesses without collapsing their distinct mandates into one generic service offer.

Mission

Connect expertise to meaningful outcomes.

OA Group brings specialist businesses into a coordinated architecture so clients can access professional capabilities with a clearer path from enquiry to execution.

Vision

Build institutions that earn trust over time.

The group aims to be recognised for professionalism, integrity, innovation and value creation across the markets it serves.
