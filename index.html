Who We Are

One group.
Five disciplines.
Unlimited capability.

OA Group was founded on the belief that Nigerian businesses deserve access to world-class professional services under a single, accountable governance structure. What began as a professional accounting practice has grown into a multi-sector group serving individuals, businesses, and government institutions across Nigeria.

Each of our five entities is independently CAC-registered, sector-specialised, and operationally distinct — yet united by shared standards of professionalism, ethics, and client-outcome focus. Whether your organisation requires statutory audit, management advisory, property investment, credit facilitation, or contract administration, OA Group provides a trusted entry point to the expertise you need.
