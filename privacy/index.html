Privacy Policy

How OA Group Nigeria handles personal information submitted through this website.

This Privacy Policy explains how OA Group Nigeria (“OA Group”, “we”, “us”) handles information provided through oagroupng.com. It is written for the website architecture specified for OA Group Nigeria and should be reviewed by the group’s appointed legal/data-protection adviser before formal publication.

Information we may collect

Information may include your name, email address, phone number, organisation, enquiry details, career application information and communications you voluntarily submit through forms.

Why we use information

We use submitted information to respond to enquiries, route requests to relevant specialist companies, communicate about recruitment, provide requested information, maintain records and operate the website.

Cookies

The website uses essential browser storage for cookie-preference handling. Non-essential cookies should only be activated where appropriate consent and the relevant technical configuration are in place.

NDPR and GDPR

OA Group operates in Nigeria and the specification requires attention to Nigeria’s data-protection framework and GDPR obligations for relevant visitors. Data rights, lawful bases, retention periods, processors and international transfers should be confirmed against OA Group’s actual processing activities before legal sign-off.

Your rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability and withdrawal of consent. Contact info@oagroupng.com for data-related requests.

Retention and security

Information should be retained only for as long as necessary for the relevant purpose and protected using appropriate technical and organisational measures.
