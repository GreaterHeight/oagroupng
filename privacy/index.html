Privacy Policy

How OA Group collects, uses, stores, and protects your personal data — in compliance with Nigeria's NDPR and international GDPR standards.

Effective Date: 1 January 2025  |  Last Updated: 1 August 2025

Introduction

OA Group (operating as OAGroupNG, www.oagroupng.com) is the parent identity for five CAC-registered Nigerian entities. This Privacy Policy governs how OA Group collects, uses, stores, and protects personal data on the oagroupng.com parent website. Each subsidiary entity maintains its own privacy practices for data collected through their individual services; clients of subsidiary entities should also review the relevant entity's privacy documentation.

This policy is designed to comply with the Nigeria Data Protection Regulation (NDPR) issued by the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA), and with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) for users accessing this site from the European Economic Area.

Data We Collect

OA Group collects the following categories of personal data on this website:

  • Identity Data: name, company name
  • Contact Data: email address, telephone number
  • Enquiry Data: service of interest, message content submitted through the contact form
  • Technical Data: IP address, browser type, device type, pages visited, time on site (collected automatically via server logs and analytics)
  • Cookie Data: preferences and session data (see Cookie Policy below)
  • Marketing Data: newsletter subscription preferences (where provided)

How We Use Your Data

OA Group uses personal data collected through this website to:

  • Respond to contact form enquiries and direct them to the appropriate entity
  • Send newsletters and updates where you have subscribed
  • Improve website performance and user experience
  • Comply with legal and regulatory obligations
  • Protect the security and integrity of the website

We process data on the following legal bases (NDPR and GDPR): consent (for marketing and non-essential cookies); legitimate interests (for enquiry handling and website analytics); legal obligation (for regulatory compliance).

Data Sharing with Entities

When you submit a contact enquiry, OA Group may share relevant information with the appropriate subsidiary entity (OA & Co, OA Consulting, Henrisol, MOL, or Pearl Primus) in order to respond to your enquiry. This sharing occurs only where necessary and proportionate to your enquiry. OA Group does not sell personal data to third parties.

Your Rights

Under the NDPR and GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access: request a copy of the personal data we hold about you
  • Correction: request correction of inaccurate personal data
  • Deletion: request deletion of your personal data, subject to legal retention requirements
  • Restriction: request restriction of processing in certain circumstances
  • Portability: receive your data in a portable format
  • Objection: object to processing based on legitimate interests
  • Withdraw Consent: withdraw consent for marketing at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact our Data Protection Officer at dpo@oagroupng.com. We will respond within 30 days.

Cookie Policy

OA Group uses cookies on oagroupng.com. Cookies are small text files stored on your device. Categories:

  • Necessary Cookies: Essential for the website to function. These cannot be disabled.
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use the site. Require your consent.
  • Marketing Cookies: Used to track and deliver relevant advertising. Require your consent.

You may manage cookie preferences at any time via the cookie banner or your browser settings.

Retention Policy

Contact form data is retained for a maximum of 24 months from the date of submission, unless an ongoing engagement relationship requires longer retention. Technical and analytics data is retained for 26 months. Marketing subscription data is retained until you unsubscribe. Data subject to legal retention requirements is retained for the legally required period.

Third-Party Services

This website may use third-party services for analytics and functionality. Any such services are selected for compliance with applicable data protection standards and are governed by appropriate data processing agreements.

Policy Updates

OA Group may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Material changes will be notified via a prominent notice on this website. The effective date at the top of this page will reflect the date of the most recent update. Continued use of oagroupng.com after a policy update constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

Contact for Data Requests

Data Protection Officer: dpo@oagroupng.com
General enquiries: info@oagroupng.com
Phone: +234 8091 999 991

If you have concerns about our data practices and we have been unable to resolve them, you may contact the National Information Technology Development Agency (NITDA) in Nigeria, or your local supervisory authority if you are based in the EEA.
