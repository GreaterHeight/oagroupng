# v4.37 — Production Form Delivery

## What changed

OA Group's Contact, Careers and newsletter forms no longer open `mailto:` links.
They now submit from the website to FormSubmit's AJAX endpoint and display an in-page success/error state.

- Contact: `https://formsubmit.co/ajax/info@oagroupng.com`
- Careers: same endpoint, with `multipart/form-data` for CV upload
- Newsletter: same endpoint as a subscription notification
- Native form fallback remains configured to `https://formsubmit.co/info@oagroupng.com`.

## Important one-time activation

FormSubmit requires the recipient email to be verified before the service will deliver submissions. The first real submission to `info@oagroupng.com` will trigger the verification/activation flow described in FormSubmit's documentation.

After activation, submissions are delivered to `info@oagroupng.com` without opening the visitor's email client.

## Careers CV

The Careers form accepts PDF, DOC and DOCX files up to 10 MB. FormSubmit documents a 10 MB combined upload limit for its file-upload workflow.

## Newsletter scope

The newsletter form now sends an actual subscription notification to `info@oagroupng.com`. It is not yet a mailing-list automation: a future phase should connect the form to a dedicated email marketing platform so confirmed subscribers are stored and campaign emails can be managed properly.

## Security / privacy notes

- The site sends only fields intentionally present in the form.
- Consent is retained as an explicit `Yes` field.
- FormSubmit CAPTCHA protection is enabled.
- The page CSP now permits HTTPS requests/forms to `formsubmit.co`.
- No email API secret is embedded in the website.
