Integrated solutions for the business lifecycle.

OA Group connects specialist companies when the client challenge is broader than a single service discipline.

Lead Routing Architecture

One institutional gateway. The right specialist destination.

Every group-level enquiry is understood first as a business requirement rather than a company preference. The group then routes the requirement to the operating company or combination of companies best positioned to respond.

This architecture protects specialist identity while making the portfolio commercially useful.
