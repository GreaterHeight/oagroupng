# 1. MASTER VISUAL DIRECTION

OA Group Nigeria should look established, intelligent, credible, Nigerian, contemporary and premium. Visuals must communicate **Integrated Expertise. Trusted Solutions. Sustainable Growth.**

Use an editorial/institutional visual language rather than generic corporate stock photography.

**Master characteristics:** photorealistic premium commercial/editorial photography; authentic contemporary Nigeria, especially Lagos where appropriate; natural Nigerian skin tones; realistic offices, architecture, infrastructure, finance and professional environments; quiet confidence rather than exaggerated wealth; controlled cinematic lighting; sophisticated composition; generous negative space.

**Approved OA Group parent palette:** `#5C6C94`, `#AAB4C8`, `#4A4A4A`, `#FFFFFF`. Do not introduce competing colours into parent OA Group imagery.

**Avoid:** visible third-party brands, logos, watermarks, invented company names, readable invented text, fake statistics, fake certificates, fake credentials, distorted anatomy, or imagery that makes unverified claims.

**Global negative prompt:** generic stock photography, cliché handshake, forced smile, people staring at camera, exaggerated luxury, fake wealth, Silicon Valley aesthetic, cyberpunk, holograms, neon, futuristic city, excessive blue glow, over-saturated colours, plastic skin, distorted anatomy, extra fingers, duplicated people, malformed hands, fake logos, readable invented text, watermarks, political propaganda, religious imagery, unrealistic Lagos skyline, fabricated landmarks, cluttered composition, low resolution, cartoon, illustration, CGI look

# 3. HOMEPAGE

## 3.1 Hero video

### `/videos/oagroup-hero.mp4`
**Placement:** Full-bleed homepage hero background.

**Generation prompt:**
Create a premium cinematic corporate motion film for **OA Group Nigeria**, a diversified Nigerian professional-services and business group. The visual language must feel institutional, intelligent, established, discreet, contemporary and premium — never flashy, futuristic, generic-tech or stock-corporate.

Open with a restrained dawn-to-morning impression of Lagos, Nigeria, using elegant aerial or architectural movement and natural atmospheric depth. Transition through realistic professional environments representing executive strategy, financial analysis, advisory work, Nigerian professionals reviewing structured business information, commercial property, infrastructure/project activity, technology-enabled operations and collaborative professional teams.

The narrative should communicate **connected expertise becoming coordinated execution**. Each scene should feel like part of one integrated business ecosystem rather than unrelated stock clips.

Use slow dolly movement, controlled lateral movement, gentle aerial drift, subtle rack focus and measured transitions. Maintain generous negative space in the left/centre-left area for the website's white hero typography. Keep important visual activity toward the right and lower-right.

Use photorealistic high-end commercial cinematography, authentic contemporary Nigeria, natural skin tones, realistic architecture and materials, premium editorial composition, restrained contrast and subtle filmic texture.

Subtly reflect the OA Group visual language through `#5C6C94`, `#AAB4C8`, `#4A4A4A` and white. No competing brand colours.

**Technical target:** 16:9, 3840×2160 master or higher, 24fps, approximately 18–24 seconds, seamless-feeling loop, calm web-safe motion, no dialogue, no embedded text, no subtitles, no watermarks.

**Negative prompt:** generic corporate stock footage, handshake clichés, people waving at camera, Silicon Valley imagery, cyberpunk, holograms, neon, excessive blue glow, fake dashboards, floating text, giant logos, watermarks, distorted hands, duplicated people, artificial faces, plastic skin, rapid cuts, shaky camera, busy central composition, political imagery, religious imagery, luxury-car flexing, unrealistic Lagos skyline

# 14. PRODUCTION / WEB DELIVERY RULES

- Generate masters at 4K/8K where supported.
- Prefer AVIF/WebP derivatives for the deployed site.
- Resize to the largest actual rendered component size plus sensible density allowance.
- Compress without visible degradation.
- Include explicit `width` and `height` attributes in HTML to prevent layout shift.
- Use `loading="lazy"` below the fold.
- Keep the hero poster optimized for first paint.
- Do not use external stock-image URLs.
- Use descriptive `alt` text based on the final image.
- Decorative images should use empty alt text where appropriate.
- Compose for the intended `.arch-frame` crop, including mobile cropping.
- Do not remove the `.arch-frame` wrapper.
