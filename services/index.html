Our Services

Six sectors. Five specialist entities. One integrated group — delivering professional services from audit and compliance to real estate, finance, and government contracting across Nigeria.

Services by sector

OA

Delivered by Olusola Adekanola & Co

Accounting & Professional Services

OA & Co brings over four decades of ICAN-accredited professional practice to every client engagement. From statutory audit to tax advisory, internal control review, and company secretariat services, the practice covers the full spectrum of financial compliance needs for Nigerian businesses, government institutions, and regulated entities. All work meets the standards required by CAC, FIRS, and applicable Nigerian regulatory bodies.

Statutory Auditing
Independent audit of financial statements in compliance with SAS and IFRS standards for CAC filing and regulatory purposes.
Internal Audit
Review of internal systems, controls, and processes to identify risk, inefficiency, and compliance gaps.
Accounting Services & Financial Reporting
Full bookkeeping, management accounts, and IFRS-compliant financial statement preparation for businesses of all sizes.
Internal Control & Compliance
Design and review of internal control frameworks to protect assets and ensure regulatory compliance.
Risk Management
Identification, assessment, and mitigation of financial, operational, and compliance risks.
Tax Services & Advisory
FIRS-compliant tax planning, computation, filing, and dispute resolution for corporations, SMEs, and individuals.
Liquidation & Insolvency
Professional management of winding-up processes, creditor representation, and asset realisation.
Company Secretariat Services
CAC filing, board resolutions, statutory registers, and corporate governance advisory.

OC

Delivered by OA Consulting Limited

Management Consulting & Advisory

OA Consulting works with private sector organisations, government MDAs, and institutional clients to improve performance, recover revenue, and build sustainable operational capability. The Revenue Recovery practice is a defining OA Consulting strength — addressing the significant leakage and undercollection that constrains many Nigerian government agencies and revenue-generating enterprises.

Business Management Advisory
Strategic and operational guidance for businesses seeking to improve performance or restructure for growth.
Revenue Generation & Recovery
Systematic identification and recovery of revenue leakage for government agencies, parastatals, and commercial organisations.
Training & Capacity Development
Customised training for executives, management teams, and professional staff across private and public sectors.
M&A Advisory
End-to-end merger, amalgamation, and acquisition advisory from deal identification through integration planning.
Procurement Management
Procurement strategy, vendor assessment, process design, and compliance review for public and private clients.
Cost & Process Optimisation
Systematic review and redesign of business processes to eliminate waste and reduce cost without compromising compliance.
Debt Recovery
Professional debt recovery advisory for organisations with significant receivables challenges, including pre-litigation strategies.

HP

Delivered by Henrisol Properties Ltd

Real Estate & Property Development

Henrisol Properties operates in one of Nigeria's most dynamic and complex investment environments. The firm provides property investment advisory, development management, and sales services — with particular focus on ensuring clients navigate the title verification, legal, and market intelligence challenges that define safe and profitable property investment in Nigeria. Diaspora investors receive dedicated service to invest securely in local property.

Real Estate Investment Advisory
Investment analysis, market intelligence, and advisory on property acquisition and portfolio return optimisation.
Property Development & Management
End-to-end development from site acquisition through construction oversight, fit-out, and ongoing management.
Facilities Management
Professional management of commercial and residential facilities — maintenance, vendor management, and tenant services.
Sales of Landed Properties
Identification, marketing, and transaction management for land sales across Lagos and key Nigerian markets.
Sales of Housing Properties
Residential property sales and acquisition advisory from off-plan purchases to completed units.
Diaspora Property Investment
Dedicated service for Nigerians abroad seeking secure property investment in Nigeria with verification and management built in.

MOL

Delivered by Maria Oyinlola Limited (MOL)

Financial Services & Microfinance

MOL addresses the persistent access-to-finance gap that constrains Nigerian economic growth. Serving individuals, SMEs, and institutional clients with credit, lending, investment advisory, and digital financing products tailored to the Nigerian financial landscape — within applicable CBN regulatory guidelines.

Credit & Lending
Structured credit products for individuals and SMEs including working capital, asset finance, and project-linked lending.
Financial Advisory
Personal and corporate financial planning, restructuring advisory, and strategic financial management support.
Investment & Asset Management
Portfolio advisory and asset management for individuals and institutions seeking managed investment exposure in Nigeria.
Agency & Revenue/Payment Distribution
Agency banking and payment distribution infrastructure for institutions requiring collection and disbursement services.
Digital Financing Solutions
Technology-enabled financing products extending credit access to digitally-active and underserved client segments.
Corporate & Institutional Financial Services
Structured financial services for corporate clients including treasury advisory, structured finance, and investment solutions.

PP

Delivered by Pearl Primus Multipurpose Contracting Limited

Contracting, Procurement & Commercial Ventures

Pearl Primus operates at the intersection of government procurement, commercial project delivery, and investment facilitation. Investment Management
Management of investment portfolios tied to commercial and infrastructure projects with structured oversight.
Contract Administration
Professional administration of government and private sector contracts through completion and final account settlement.
Project Facilitation
Coordination, stakeholder management, and facilitation for complex multi-party projects requiring structured oversight.
Government Procurement (BPP-compliant)
BPP registration advisory, bid preparation support, and compliance management for government contracting.
Procurement Management
End-to-end procurement from supplier qualification through tendering, evaluation, award, and monitoring.
Commercial Ventures
Identification, structuring, and execution of commercial partnerships, joint ventures, and investment opportunities.
